## Interactive Simulations (McCreary)

These browser-based simulations let you explore Module 5 concepts hands-on. All simulations are from Dan McCreary's [Introduction to Economics](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/) (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

| Simulation | What It Teaches | Try It |
|---|---|---|
| Network Effects Visualizer | Add users to a network and see how value grows non-linearly with each new connection | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/network-effects/) |
| Platform Economics Simulator | Balance supply and demand on a two-sided marketplace; see how tipping points emerge | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/platform-economics/) |
| Compound Interest Visualizer | Adjust growth rates and time horizons to see how small differences compound into large gaps | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/compound-interest/) |
| Nominal vs. Real GDP | Adjust for inflation and see why economists insist on real GDP for comparing across years | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/nominal-vs-real-gdp/) |
| Attention Economy Calculator | Explore how digital platforms compete for your time and how attention becomes a scarce resource | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/attention-economy/) |

## Videos

1. **"Moneyball — Economics of Sports Analytics"** — YouTube, ~12 min. Explains the economic logic behind sabermetrics and its parallels to productivity measurement.
2. **Robert Solow's Nobel Prize Lecture (1987)** — [nobelprize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1987/solow/lecture/), ~45 min. Solow explains the growth model and the technology residual in his own words.
3. **"Why Are Some Countries Rich and Others Poor?"** — Marginal Revolution University, ~10 min. Concise introduction to the Solow model, capital accumulation, and the role of institutions.
4. **"India's UPI Revolution Explained"** — YouTube, ~15 min. How India built the world's largest real-time digital payment system, illustrating network effects and platform economics.
5. **"Creative Destruction: Schumpeter Explained"** — Economics Explained, YouTube, ~12 min. An accessible introduction to Schumpeter's theory with modern examples.
