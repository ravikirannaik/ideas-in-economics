# Module 1 Resources: Understanding Economic Thinking

## Interactive Simulations (McCreary)

These browser-based simulations let you explore Module 1 concepts hands-on. All simulations are from Dan McCreary's [Introduction to Economics](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/) (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

| Simulation | What It Teaches | Try It |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity Cost Calculator | Enter choices and see the value of what you give up | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/opportunity-cost-calculator/) |
| Production Possibilities Frontier | Drag the production point along the PPF to visualize trade-offs | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/ppf-explorer/) |
| Economic Systems Comparison | Compare how market, command, and mixed systems allocate resources | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/economic-systems-spectrum/) |
| Correlation vs. Causation Detector | Practice distinguishing genuine causal claims from spurious correlations | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/correlation-causation/) |
| Diminishing Marginal Benefit | Watch how the benefit of each additional pizza slice declines | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/marginal-benefit-pizza/) |

## Economist Stories (McCreary)

Biographical narratives of economists whose work connects to this module's themes.

| Economist | Key Insight | Read It |
|---|---|---|
| Adam Smith — The Wealth of Watching | How observation of ordinary life led to the foundations of economics | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/stories/adam-smith/) |
| Ibn Khaldun — The Muqaddimah | A medieval scholar who described supply, demand, and taxation centuries before Smith | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/stories/ibn-khaldun/) |
| Harriet Martineau — The Woman Who Translated Wealth | The writer who made economics accessible to ordinary readers through storytelling | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/stories/harriet-martineau/) |
| Thomas Sowell — Basic Economics | An economist who explains complex concepts through real-world incentives and common sense | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/stories/thomas-sowell/) |

## Videos

1. **"The Big Short — Economics Explained"** — YouTube, ~15 min. Breaks down the economic mechanisms behind the film.
2. **Richard Thaler's Nobel Prize Lecture** — [nobelprize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2017/thaler/lecture/), 45 min. Thaler explains behavioral economics in his own words.
3. **"What is Economics?"** — Marginal Revolution University, ~10 min. Concise introduction to scarcity, trade-offs, and incentives.
