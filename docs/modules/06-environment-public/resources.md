# Module 6 Resources: Environmental and Public Economics

---

## Interactive Simulations (McCreary)

These browser-based simulations let you explore Module 6 concepts hands-on. All simulations are from Dan McCreary's [Introduction to Economics](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/) (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

| Simulation | What It Teaches | Try It |
|---|---|---|
| Negative Externality Visualizer | Shows how pollution causes the market to overproduce — the gap between private cost and social cost is the externality | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/negative-externality/) |
| Public Goods Game | Simulates the free rider problem: each player decides whether to contribute to a shared fund, revealing why voluntary provision fails | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/public-goods-game/) |
| Tragedy of the Commons | Models how a shared resource (fishery, pasture) is depleted when each user acts in self-interest without governance | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/tragedy-commons/) |
| Tax Incidence Explorer | Adjust supply and demand elasticities to see how the burden of a Pigouvian tax shifts between buyers and sellers | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/tax-incidence-explorer/) |
| Policy Claim Evaluator | Practice evaluating the logic and evidence behind common economic policy claims — including environmental and tax policy arguments | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/policy-evaluator/) |
| Feedback Loops | Visualizes positive and negative feedback loops — crucial for understanding how environmental degradation can create self-reinforcing cycles | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/feedback-loops/) |

## Videos

1. **Elinor Ostrom's Nobel Prize Lecture** — [nobelprize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/lecture/), ~45 min. Ostrom explains her life's work on commons governance in her own words.
2. **"Externalities — Economics Explained"** — YouTube, ~12 min. Clear visual explanation of negative and positive externalities with real-world examples.
3. **"Tragedy of the Commons — Explained"** — Marginal Revolution University, ~8 min. Concise introduction to the commons problem and its solutions.
4. **"Carbon Tax vs. Cap-and-Trade"** — Resources for the Future, ~10 min. Compares the two main market-based approaches to controlling emissions.
5. **"The Story of Stuff"** — YouTube, ~21 min. Traces the environmental externalities of consumer goods from extraction to disposal.
