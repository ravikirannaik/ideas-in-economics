## Interactive Simulations (McCreary)

These browser-based simulations let you explore Module 4 concepts hands-on. All simulations are from Dan McCreary's [Introduction to Economics](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/) (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

| Simulation | What It Teaches | Try It |
|---|---|---|
| GDP Calculator | Compute GDP using the expenditure approach and see what economic activity gets measured — and what falls outside | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/gdp-calculator/) |
| GDP Components Breakdown | Visualize how consumption, investment, government spending, and net exports divide a country's economic output | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/gdp-components-pie/) |
| Efficiency-Equity Spectrum | Explore the trade-off between maximizing total economic output and distributing it fairly across the population | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/efficiency-equity-spectrum/) |
| Human Capital ROI Calculator | Estimate the return on investment from additional years of education and see how education returns vary | [:material-open-in-new: Open](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/sims/human-capital-roi/) |

## Videos

1. **Esther Duflo's Nobel Prize Lecture** — [nobelprize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2019/duflo/lecture/), ~35 min. Duflo explains the experimental approach to poverty alleviation in her own words.
2. **Abhijit Banerjee's Nobel Prize Lecture** — [nobelprize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2019/banerjee/lecture/), ~35 min. Banerjee on what RCTs have taught us about the economic lives of the poor.
3. **"How to Fight Poverty" — Esther Duflo, TED Talk** — [ted.com](https://www.ted.com/talks/esther_duflo_social_experiments_to_fight_poverty), ~17 min. An accessible introduction to the RCT approach with examples from India and Africa.
4. **"The India Story — NITI Aayog on Multidimensional Poverty"** — YouTube, ~20 min. India's progress on the MPI, with state-level breakdowns.
5. **"Slumdog Millionaire — Economics in Film"** — YouTube, ~12 min. Analysis of economic themes in the film, including the informal economy and social mobility.
