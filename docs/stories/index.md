# Economist Stories

These biographical narratives are from Dan McCreary's [Introduction to Economics](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/) open textbook, licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Each story provides an accessible introduction to the life, context, and key contributions of an influential economist.

The stories are organized below by the course module they most closely support. Several economists are relevant to more than one module --- their primary placement reflects the strongest thematic fit.
