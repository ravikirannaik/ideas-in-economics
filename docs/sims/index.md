# Interactive Simulations

These browser-based simulations are from Dan McCreary's [Introduction to Economics](https://dmccreary.github.io/economics-course/) open textbook, licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). They run entirely in your browser --- no installation or login required.

Each simulation lets you adjust inputs and observe how economic models respond. They are organized below by the course module they most closely support. Click any link to open the simulation in a new tab.

---

*Scarcity, opportunity cost, trade-offs, economic systems, and thinking like an economist.*
